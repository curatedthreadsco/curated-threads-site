## Why every-day gifts land better

The hunter in your life already owns their gear. They chose it themselves and they will keep buying it themselves. What they do not have is the small every-day pieces that show up alongside the coffee, in the tumbler on the tailgate, or on the hoodie they grab on the way out the door in November. Those are the gifts that get used year round.
